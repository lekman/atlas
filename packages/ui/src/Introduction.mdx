# Atlas

A live map of your agents, your clients and the work between them, drawn as
a world you choose.

Every story here runs on an invented dataset: a consultancy called Ashgrove
and four clients that do not exist. This Storybook is public, so nothing in
it comes from real work.

## Worlds

| World      | Home       | Clients      | Agents  | Open questions | Decisions     |
| ---------- | ---------- | ------------ | ------- | -------------- | ------------- |
| Astronomer | Terra      | star systems | probes  | comets         | charted stars |
| Mariner    | Turtle Bay | islands      | vessels | fog banks      | lighthouses   |

Use the **World** control in the toolbar to see a component in either world.
Status colours are the same in every world: green running, blue watching,
amber needs you, red blocked, grey idle.

## Where to start

- **Pages / Atlas** shows the whole view, in the sidebar and full page.
- **Worlds / World Canvas** shows one kind of event at a time.
- **Components** holds the panels on their own.
